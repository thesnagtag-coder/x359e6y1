Why you have this

Welcome. Here's exactly what this does for you.

Chinese New Year brings firecrackers, lanterns and string lights, and a reunion dinner table that runs for hours with grandparents and children in the same busy kitchen. These cause real harm every year, and until now there's never been an easy way for an everyday host to check for them.

This isn't about fear. It's the same hazard-and-control thinking used on licensed worksites, built by an HSE professional (NEBOSH/OSHA trained) and simplified so you can use it in under five minutes before hosting anything, big or small.
